# References

Effect sizes in the data were set by hand with these papers in mind. The data were not fitted to them, or to any real dataset.

## Heat, cold and pregnancy

- Chersich MF et al. Associations between high temperatures in pregnancy and risk of preterm birth, low birth weight, and stillbirths: systematic review and meta-analysis. BMJ 2020. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.m3811
- Lakhoo DP et al. A systematic review and meta-analysis of heat exposure impacts on maternal, fetal and neonatal health. Nat Med 2024. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03395-8
- Raghavan S et al. Association between heat and air pollution (PM2.5 and black carbon) exposure in pregnancy and preterm birth in low- and middle-income countries. BMJ Paediatr Open 2025. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2025-003428
- Khosravipour M et al. Short-term ambient temperature variations and incidence of preterm birth: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Int J Hyg Environ Health 2024. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijheh.2023.114319
- Ruan T et al. Association between low ambient temperature during pregnancy and adverse birth outcomes: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Chin Med J 2023. https://doi.org/10.1097/CM9.0000000000002361
- Nyadanu SD et al. Maternal exposure to ambient air temperature and adverse birth outcomes: an umbrella review of systematic reviews and meta-analyses. Sci Total Environ 2024. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2024.170236

Metadata for the papers above is from PubMed.
