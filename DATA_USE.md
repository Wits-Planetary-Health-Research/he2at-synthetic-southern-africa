# Data use note

The data in `data/` are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (full text in `LICENSE-DATA`). The code in `examples/`, `tools/` and `notebooks/` is under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`). Anyone may use, share and adapt the files, for any purpose.

- Attribution (required): cite HE²AT Center, Wits Planetary Health Research as the source in slides, code and papers. `CITATION.cff` has the reference.
- Synthetic data: every value is simulated. No record corresponds to a real person and no weather day corresponds to a real day. Results are about methods, not about real women, real places or real HE²AT cohorts, and should not be presented as findings about them.
- Not for clinical or policy decisions: do not use these files, or results from them, to make decisions about patients, health services or policy.

Two requests, not conditions of the licence:

- If you develop code with this data, we would be glad to see it. Send it to Craig Parker (craig.parker@witsphr.org).
- If a paper comes out of work on this data, please get in touch.
